Year 6 Mathematics · Statistics and Probability · Term 3–4

Buddy Games Day

How can we use chance and data to design, run and honestly evaluate a Games Day for our Prep buddies?

Fifteen lessons. Eight on probability before the break, seven on statistics after it, and a real event in between.

How the unit fits together

Year 6 teams design a chance game, run it for their Prep buddies, then analyse what actually happened. The two halves are assessed as one folio.
